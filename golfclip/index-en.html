About the app

GolfClip takes a video you recorded continuously at the range, uses on-device AI to detect the moment of each swing, and clips it out into its own file — automatically flagging likely practice swings along the way.

FAQ

What languages are supported?

Japanese and English.

Is my video data sent anywhere?

No. All video analysis and clipping happens on your device — nothing is sent to a server. If you enable the PRO Google Drive backup, clips are uploaded directly to your own Google account. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Does Google Drive backup access my other files?

No. The app uses a restricted permission (the drive.file scope) that only lets it access files it creates itself — it cannot see your existing files or folders.
